About Bapa Hotel

Bapa Hotel welcomes guests to Bobi Trading Centre in Patoo Village, Omoro District, northern Uganda. It is a place to stay while visiting Bobi and the surrounding area.

Frequently asked questions

        

        
        

          

            Where is Bapa Hotel located?
            
Bapa Hotel is at Bobi Trading Centre, Patoo Village, Omoro District, Uganda. A public listing describes it as about 500 metres from the Gulu–Kampala Highway, so please confirm that with the hotel before you travel.

          

          

            How can I enquire about accommodation?
            
Use the enquiry form on this page, call +256 782 264973, or message the hotel on WhatsApp. The form prepares a WhatsApp message that you review and send yourself.

          

          

            How do I contact the hotel?
            
By phone or WhatsApp on +256 782 264973. No other contact details are listed here until the hotel confirms them.

          

          

            Does sending an enquiry confirm a reservation?
            
No. An enquiry does not reserve a room or guarantee availability. Your stay is confirmed only after the hotel replies and agrees the details with you.

          

          

            How can I get directions to the hotel?
            
Use the Get Directions button to open Google Maps with a search for the hotel's address. The pin may not be exact, so call the hotel if you need help finding it.

          

          

            What are the room rates?
            
Rates depend on the room and your dates and are confirmed by the hotel. Please contact the hotel for current rates.

          

          

            What about check-in times, payment and cancellation?
            
These details have not been confirmed for this website. Please ask the hotel when you enquire.

          

          

            Which facilities does the hotel have?
            
Public listings mention a swimming pool, a bar and food service. Please confirm what is currently available when you enquire.
